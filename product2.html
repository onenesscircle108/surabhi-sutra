Application Ritual

Three Steps. Twice a Day.

01
Wet & Activate

Dampen your face with lukewarm water. Take a small amount (a 5-rupee-coin portion) and add a few drops of water to activate the botanical powders.

02
Massage Gently

Using circular motions, massage the paste across your face for 60 seconds. The gentle grit exfoliates while the turmeric penetrates. Take your time — this is your ritual.

03
Rinse & Reveal

Rinse with cool water to close pores. Pat dry gently. Follow with toner or moisturiser while skin is still slightly damp for maximum absorption.

Important Notes

Safety & Storage

Who Should Use This?+
Safe for all skin types including sensitive skin. Ideal for ages 14 and above. Suitable for Indian skin tones and climates. Tested for year-round use across all seasons.
Patch Test Advice+
Apply a small amount to the inner wrist or behind the ear. Wait 24 hours. If no redness or irritation appears, proceed with full use.
Pregnancy & Breastfeeding+
All ingredients are natural and generally safe. Please consult your doctor before use during pregnancy or breastfeeding — standard guidance for any new skincare product.
Storage & Shelf Life+
Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Shelf life is 12 months from manufacture. Avoid storing in humid bathrooms. Use within 6 months of opening.
