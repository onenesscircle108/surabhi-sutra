Research-Backed

Science Behind Cellular Brightening

Melanin Pathway Regulation

Curcuminoids in wild turmeric inhibit tyrosinase — the enzyme responsible for melanin synthesis — at the source, preventing tan and dark spots before they form.

Accelerated Cell Turnover

Lentil saponins gently accelerate the natural desquamation (shedding) process, revealing fresh epidermal cells underneath at a healthier, more consistent rate.

Oxidative Defence

Polyphenols from sandalwood and turmeric neutralise free radicals generated by UV exposure and pollution, protecting cellular DNA from oxidative damage.

Inflammation Modulation

Curcumin's anti-inflammatory action downregulates NF-κB — a key inflammation signalling molecule — reducing post-acne redness and chronic skin inflammation.

Barrier Health

Honey's humectant properties draw moisture into the skin while creating a protective film that prevents transepidermal water loss (TEWL) after cleansing.
