THE PHILOSOPHY

Why Surabhi Sutra Exists

We started Surabhi Sutra because we were tired of reading ingredient lists we couldn't pronounce. Raised on Ayurvedic home remedies, we knew that turmeric, neem, and rose water worked — not because of marketing, but because generations of women had proved it. Every product we make starts with a question: what would a wise grandmother recommend? Then we listen.
